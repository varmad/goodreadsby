# Book Recommendations

A site that helps people choose their next book by recording who recommended what, when
they said it, and always showing the evidence. It competes on freshness: recommendations
surface within days of being made, not years. Revenue comes from Amazon affiliate links.

## Language

### The facts

**Recommendation**:
The atomic fact of the domain: a named person spoke positively about a specific book, at
a knowable moment, evidenced by a citable public source. Positive by definition — a
neutral or negative remark is not a Recommendation. Every page on the site is assembled
from these.
_Avoid_: review, rating, mention, shoutout, endorsement

**Recommender**:
A real, identifiable person whose public book recommendations we record. Usually the
guest on a Feed, not its host.
_Avoid_: influencer, celebrity, popular face, expert, curator

**List**:
A named view over Recommendations, assembled by query. Not a document anyone authors
or stores.
_Avoid_: collection, shelf, roundup, compilation

### The evidence

**Feed**:
A recurring publisher of Sources that we monitor continuously — a podcast, a channel, an
interview series. Orthogonal to Recommender: one Feed hosts many Recommenders, and one
Recommender appears across many Feeds.
_Avoid_: show, channel, publisher, subscription

**Source**:
A single published artifact that evidences Recommendations — one podcast episode, one
interview, one article. Cites the exact position within itself, such as a timestamp. A
Recommendation without a Source does not exist.
_Avoid_: citation, reference, proof, link, episode

### The catalogue

**Work**:
A book as a thing people talk about, independent of how it is printed or sold.
Recommendations point at Works, and Works are what we count and deduplicate.
_Avoid_: book, title, item

**Edition**:
One purchasable form of a Work — a specific format and printing, with its own
identifiers. Editions are what we link to and earn commission on.
_Avoid_: version, copy, product, variant

### The pipeline

**Candidate**:
An extracted claim that a person recommended a book, awaiting human judgement. Becomes a
Recommendation only if a human approves it, and is discarded otherwise. Never public.
_Avoid_: draft, pending recommendation, suggestion, proposal

**Retraction**:
The withdrawal of a published Recommendation found to be wrong or disputed. The
Recommendation leaves every public view but the record survives, along with why it was
withdrawn and who approved it in the first place.
_Avoid_: delete, removal, takedown, unpublish
